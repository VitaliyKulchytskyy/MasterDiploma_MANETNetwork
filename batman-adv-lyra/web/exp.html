How it works

Leave the other node in place: it answers test messages by itself (the echo responder runs on every node). Carry this node to each test point, enter the point and press Start. Wi-Fi: pings to the other node through the mesh, with signal and link quality every second. ERL: numbered test messages that the other node echoes back. Low load: 100 pings every 1 s and 10 messages every 30 s (about 5 min). High load: 1000 pings every 50 ms and 30 messages every 2 s (about 1.5 min).
